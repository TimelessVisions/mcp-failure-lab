# Public-safe demo — storyboard

Nine shots, one per stage of the public-safe walkthrough, driven by
`timeline.json`. The capture asserts each stage id, so the recording and the
script cannot drift.

| # | t | Stage id | On screen | Held because |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 00:00 | `public-what-this-is` | The synthetic banner, the disclaimer, and what *is* real about the demo. | Longest opening of either demo. Everything after it depends on this landing. |
| 2 | 00:22 | `public-problem` | The problem statement. | The one part of the subject matter that is not synthetic. |
| 3 | 00:42 | `public-shape` | Counts, with the synthetic note repeated beneath them. | First stage showing numbers, so the note reappears. |
| 4 | 01:02 | `public-applicability` | A `NOT_APPLICABLE` cell and the dated-profile rule. | The strongest honest claim available publicly. |
| 5 | 01:26 | `public-outcome-vs-security` | Outcome, security relevance and confirmation as separate ideas. | Needs time so "not a finding" is read, not assumed. |
| 6 | 01:48 | `public-signature` | What complete signature equality means. | Pure methodology; no result is asserted. |
| 7 | 02:10 | `public-timings` | A timing card with its full sample strip. | The samples are the argument. |
| 8 | 02:30 | `public-coverage` | The coverage ledger, labelled as timing metrics. | Distinguishes the two not-measured states. |
| 9 | 02:48 | `public-refusals` | What the tool refuses to say, and that the private benchmark is absent. | Ends on a limitation and a boundary. |

## The synthetic note

Stages 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 each carry the note in-frame, not only in stage 1. This
is deliberate redundancy: a still taken from the middle of the recording is the
most likely thing to be reused, and it has to carry its own context.

Stage 9 restates the disclaimer in full as the closing frame.

## Framing

Identical to the private demo — 1920×1080, scale factor 1, no cursor, no zoom,
no added transitions, no terminal — so the two recordings are visibly the same
piece of work at different visibilities. Both masters (standard and
reduced-motion) come from one capture run.

## What is deliberately not in shot

- The private benchmark, in any form, including as a redacted or blurred version.
- Anything that would let a viewer infer that a specific real implementation was
  measured, such as a distinctive version string or an unusual capability shape.
- Any repository, branch, workflow, artifact URL or file path.
